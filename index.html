Feature guide

Smart guides & snapping. While dragging an object, magenta lines appear when its edges or centre line up with nearby objects, room edges or wall faces — it snaps to them so things stay aligned. Green dashed lines show live clearances to the surrounding room walls.

Right-click menu. Right-click any object for Duplicate, Rotate ±90°, Align to nearest wall, Bring to front / Send to back, Copy and Delete. Right-click empty canvas for Paste and Fit.

Resize & rotate. A selected object shows white corner handles (drag to resize — hold ⇧ or turn on Lock proportions to keep the aspect ratio) and a round handle above it to rotate. Drag a wall's endpoints to resize it, or its round handle to rotate.

Doors & windows snap to the nearest wall automatically when dropped, and open/close via the inspector (swing angle, hinge side, sliding/garage amount).

3D views. Dollhouse / Top / Front presets, cutaway or full-height walls, show/hide roof, and a first-person Walk-through button. The Sun & shadows panel drives a real day→night cycle from time-of-day, date and location.

Saving. Work autosaves to this browser. Use Export JSON to keep a portable copy, and Import JSON to bring it back or move it to another machine.

2. Beam Depth & Deflection Control

Beam depth is governed by deflection limits to prevent ceiling cracking and sagging:

Basic L/d limit: Simply Supported ≤ 20 | Continuous ≤ 26 | Cantilever ≤ 7

Rule of Thumb: Depth $D \approx \text{Clear Span} / 12$ to $\text{Clear Span} / 15$. Width $b = 23\text{ cm}$ (9″) to avoid protruding offsets from standard masonry walls.

3. Tributary Weight Breakdown

Total design dead and live loads calculated on the floor structure:

  • RCC Slab (150mm): $0.15\text{ m} \times 25\text{ kN/m}^3 = 3.75\text{ kN/m}^2$
  • Floor Finishes (Tiles, Screed, Plaster): $1.0\text{ kN/m}^2$
  • Residential Live Load (IS 875 Part 2): $2.0\text{ kN/m}^2$
  • 230mm Masonry Wall (3m height): $\approx 13.1\text{ kN/m}$
  • Safety Load Factor: $1.5 \times (DL + LL)$
