Idea 2 · retain the rooms

18 aligned columns per storey, 23 main beams and 18 cantilever stubs. Width grid matches Idea 1; depth bays are 4.23 m. Outer longitudinal beams are omitted on both sides to clear the rotated stair; slab edges and collectors need explicit design.

Engineering handoff

Coordination envelopes are 450 × 450 mm columns, 300 × 450 mm beams, 150 mm slab and 200 mm RC wall zones. These are not selected design sizes; reinforcement is intentionally unspecified. A 3.048 m storey with a 450 mm beam leaves about 2.60 m before finishes/services below beams; confirm the parking clearance and stair headroom.

Idea 1 preliminary future-G+2 tributary bookkeeping gives a maximum column service reaction of about 674 kN. At the unverified 150 kPa bearing assumption, adding an illustrative 10% allowance gives a square bearing-only trial around 2.22 m. This excludes detailed stair/chajja loads and retains conservative masonry/RC overlap; it is not a complete design load. It does not check bending, punching, settlement, uplift, wall overturning, adjacent foundations or excavation. The app does not model RC wall stiffness. No seismic drift/pass result is claimed.

Future flexibility applies to lightweight partitions within a design load allowance; columns, RC walls, beams, stair openings and major wet areas remain fixed. Future storeys, heavy masonry changes, tanks, equipment, terrace crowds and gardens require explicit loading/design. Foundation and future-storey detailing must be approved before constructing the ground floor.

Confirm survey boundaries/road level, soil investigation, local setbacks, actual building location and governing standards with the appointed engineer.
