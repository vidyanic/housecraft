# Three-sided U stair with floor continuity

Clear room 11 feet 6 inches × 9 feet 6 inches; floor-to-floor rise 10 feet. Entry platform is 3 × 3 feet; the exit is a full-width 3-foot-deep upper bridge landing, plus two level corner landings. The longer-side start climbs clockwise on both storeys: 18 equal 6.67-inch risers in 7 + 4 + 7 flights, uniform 11-inch going and 40.5 × 36-inch corner landings. Shorter-side start: 17 equal 7.06-inch risers in 5 + 7 + 5 flights, 10.5-inch going and 37.5 × 36-inch corner landings.

Import either JSON into Atelier Plan as a separate project. Each has ground, first and second floors at 0, 10 and 20 feet. The first-floor flights keep the same direction. The full-width bridge connects the ground stair exit corner to the first-floor entrance corner, avoiding an opposing-flight headroom collision. Room floor finishes are cut away across the derived stairwell opening; the blue arrival platform stays visible. Use all-floor 3D stacking to inspect continuity.

Change Start along or Start corner in Properties to recalculate. Width, length, rise, clear flight width, minimum corner landing, entrance/exit platform length and preferred riser are editable. Connect/add upper floor creates the arrival connection; View upper arrival navigates there. In that floor’s Project properties, Continue to next floor creates a stair with the same flight direction and a further arrival floor in one undo action. Floor elevation changes reconcile the linked rise when feasible, otherwise disconnect the link. Copy-to-floor clears the old arrival link.

Counts denote risers: each flight has one fewer tread before its corner landing/exit. Equal rises sum exactly to the floor height. The geometric search uses 14–18 cm risers, 25–33 cm going, 60–65 cm for 2R+T, and 3–12 risers per flight. This is a comfort/layout heuristic, not compliance or structural design. Openings affect architectural floor finishes, not structural slab/load calculations; explicit structural slabs and floor openings require independent design. Confirm 2.2 m headroom, guard continuity, clear widths including rails/finishes, and door clearance. Requested 3 feet (0.9144 m) is below the common 1 m dwelling width benchmark.
